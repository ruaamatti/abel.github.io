Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is a phishing email?

A phishing email is a deceptive message designed to trick someone into sharing information, opening an attachment, or following a harmful link. Check unexpected messages carefully.

How do I report suspicious emails?

In Outlook, select the message, choose Report, and then choose Report Phishing. Follow your organization’s usual reporting process if you are unsure.

What happens if I click a simulated phishing email?

The scoring rules deduct points for clicking a simulated phishing link. Complete the recommended training to earn the training points and practice safer habits for next time.

How are scores calculated?

Everyone starts with 100 points. Reporting, clicking, training, reporting streaks, and reporting order affect the score. See the full scoring rules for details.
