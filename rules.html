How to participate

Derby rules

Practice the same careful habits you would use with any unexpected email. The derby is a learning activity, not a test of personal information.

01

What to expect

During the campaign, participants will receive 2-3 simulated phishing emails. Treat every unexpected message the same way you would a real phishing attempt: pause, check the sender and message, avoid clicking unfamiliar links or opening unexpected attachments, and report anything suspicious.

These simulations are designed for awareness and practice. If you interact with one, use it as a chance to learn and complete the recommended training.

02

How to report

In Outlook, select the suspicious message and use the built-in phishing reporting option:

Outlook → Report → Report Phishing

Do not reply to the message or forward it to other participants. If you are unsure whether an email is part of the simulation, report it through your organization’s usual process.

03

Scoring system

Everyone starts with 100 points. Points reflect safe choices and reporting during the campaign.

Points for each simulated phishing email
